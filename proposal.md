# RYDESAVYR — Project Proposal

## Overview
RYDESAVYR is a Python project developed as part of the Columbia Python Bootcamp. RYDESAVYR helps people get home smartly by comparing every way home and recommending the best one for their situation.

## Problem Statement
Picture this: it's midnight, you're trying to get home, you don't know exactly where you are, and your phone is at 10% battery. In that moment you don't have time to open five different apps and compare prices, wait times, and routes — you just need the best option, fast.

RYDESAVYR solves this by collecting all the ways to get home (rideshare, taxi, public transit, walking, biking, scooters, etc.) in one place and surfacing the best option based on what matters most to the user in that moment.

## Goals
- Aggregate all available "get home" options (rideshare, taxi, transit, walk, bike, scooter, etc.) into a single view
- Rank and recommend options based on multiple weighted factors:
  - Price
  - Time
  - Distance
  - Energy (personal effort/battery life required)
  - Nature-vibez (scenic/pleasant routes)
  - Carbon footprint
  - Morality (e.g., safety, ethical considerations of the option)
- Work fast and reliably under low-battery, low-information conditions (minimal taps, minimal data needed)
- Let users customize how much each factor matters to them
